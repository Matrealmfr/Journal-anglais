What AI costs the planet

Generative AIRunning on Power

AI uses electricity and water. We still don’t know exactly how much.

Data centers

Data centers are big buildings full of computers. They help AI work. In North America, they now need much more electricity than before.

In 2022, all data centers in the world used 460 TWh of electricity. That is a huge amount. Experts think it could reach about 1,050 TWh by 2026.

Training vs. everyday use

Teaching an AI is like a very long homework session. It needs a lot of power. One study said teaching one AI model used as much electricity as 120 American homes use in a year.

Using AI also costs energy. Each time you ask a chatbot a question, the computer works to answer. One ChatGPT question uses about 5 times more electricity than a normal web search.

The hidden cost

When you ask AI something, you don’t see the energy it uses. The app looks simple, but the power is still used.

Water

Computers get very hot. Data centers use cold water to cool them down, like a drink for the machines. For every unit of power they use, they need about two liters of water.

In places where water is scarce, this can take water away from people and nature. The UN warns that by 2030, AI could use twice as much energy and water.

Hardware

AI needs special chips called GPUs. They are harder to make than normal computer chips. Making them and moving them also uses energy and materials.

In 2023, three companies sent about 3.85 million GPUs to data centers. In 2022, it was about 2.67 million.

What is Arcep?

Arcep is France’s official watchdog for phones, internet and networks. In June 2026, it looked at how AI affects the environment.

Its main message: the strongest AI is not always the most power-hungry. Using AI wisely does not have to mean worse answers.

It sets four goals and nine ideas, such as shared rules for measuring AI’s energy use.

We need to measure AI’s cost better.

The good side

Some cities reuse data-center heat. In Odense, Denmark, Meta says its data center heats about 11,000 homes (1). AI can also warn people early about storms.

The bad side

Data centers use water to stay cool, which can strain dry areas (2). AI needs lots of electricity, often from fossil fuels (3). Making its chips adds pollution too (3).
